Every rep, every meal, every hour of sleep: planned.

People ask me how I stay disciplined. The honest answer: I don't rely on discipline alone. I rely on a plan.

Motivation is not a plan

Motivation is great on Monday morning. By Thursday it's gone. If your results depend on how you feel, they will come and go with your mood.

So I don't ask myself whether I feel like it. I already know what the day looks like.

What my day looks like

I wake up at 5. Water first, then coffee, then a walk around my neighbourhood. Shower. My first meal is eggs and rice. Then I go to work.

My food is simple. Rice, chicken, eggs. Breakfast and usually four more meals. Some people would call it boring. I call it quality. It gives my body what it needs, every day, without guessing.

In the evening I walk again. I go to bed around nine or ten. When the alarm rings at 5, my body has recovered.

Nothing in this day is special. That is exactly the point. It repeats.

Food is the foundation, not half

Most people work hard in the gym and then walk into the kitchen and everything falls apart. They eat irregularly. They forget to drink. They sleep whenever.

The biggest mistake I see: people are afraid of food. They want to lose weight, so they stop eating. That is the worst decision you can make. Your metabolism slows down and your energy goes with it.

Think of it like this. If you don't put fuel in the car, it won't go. If you stop adding wood, the fire goes out. Your body works the same way.

Food is not only about not being hungry. It is your performance, your nutrients and your recovery. Water is not only about not being thirsty. It keeps your body working. Sleep is when you actually grow.

Everything is connected. Every detail matters.

The other 23 hours

When I train a client, I see them for one hour. The other 23 hours they are on their own. That is where the result is made.

Discipline is not what you do when the coach is watching. It's what you do when nobody is watching. You don't do it for your coach. You do it for yourself, for your health and for your mindset.

No shortcuts

There are no shortcuts in this sport. What you put in, you get back. Hard work, real results. Half-hearted work, nothing.

I compete natural. It's the harder road, and I know it works. Not overnight, but it works.

And when a day goes wrong, it doesn't matter. If Monday fails, Tuesday is a new start. Try again, from a different angle. Every day is a new beginning.

Built for one person

Everyone is different: how they think, how they live, what drives them. That's why I don't hand out templates. Your plan is built around your training, your food, your sleep and your life.

The goal is not to need me forever. The goal is that you own the system.

Do what you love, and love what you do.
