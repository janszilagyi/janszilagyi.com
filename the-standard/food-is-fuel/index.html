Food is fuel, not the enemy.

The most common mistake I see is not lazy training. It is an empty plate.

Someone decides to lose weight. The first thing they do is eat less. Then less again. Skip breakfast. Skip dinner. They think hunger means progress.

It doesn't.

When you don't give your body enough food, it protects itself. Your metabolism slows down. Your energy drops. Your training gets weaker. Recovery takes longer. You lose strength, not just weight. And the results you wanted move further away.

A car without fuel won't go. It doesn't matter how good the car is. It doesn't matter how good the driver is. No fuel, no movement.

Your body works the same way.

Food is not half of the result

People ask me what matters more – training or food. My answer is simple. Food is the foundation. Not half. The foundation.

You can train hard every day. If you don't eat right, you will not get back what you put in. Training breaks the body down. Food builds it back up. Without the second part, the first part is just tiredness.

Think of a fire. Without wood, it goes out. You can wish for it all you want. It still needs wood. Every day.

What I eat

My food is simple. Rice. Chicken. Eggs. Quality, not complexity. I don't need fancy meals to stay on track. I need the right meals, at the right time, every day.

There are no shortcuts. What you put in, you get back. That is true for training, and it is even more true for food.

Everything is connected

Food never works alone. Training, food, water, sleep, recovery – it is one system. If one part is missing, the whole thing suffers. You can't recover on an empty stomach. Every detail matters.

That is why I wake at five, walk in the morning and in the evening, eat on time and go to bed early. Not because someone is watching. Because the body I want is built in those details.

Every plan is personal

I won't tell you exactly how much to eat. Nobody can do that from an article. Everyone is unique. Your body, your goal, your day – the plan has to fit you, not someone else.

But I can tell you this: if your plan is built on starving, it is the wrong plan.

Start again today

Maybe you have been under-eating for weeks. Maybe you skipped meals this week and you feel flat. That's fine. Every day is a new start. If Monday failed, Tuesday you start again.

Eat. Train. Sleep. Repeat. Give your body what it needs, and it will give you back more than you expect.

Food is fuel. Not the enemy.

Do what you love, and love what you do.
